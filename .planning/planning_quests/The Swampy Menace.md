`Requirements:` [[Kill it with Fire]] and [[Ball of a Boy]]
`Starting point:` Speak to the Guard Captain
`Reward: `
6,000 health experience
3,000 coins


1. Speaking to the guard captain starts the quest. We are tasked to speak to Oolga and do whatever she asks so that she can cure the disease discovered in the rat den in the sewers. 
	1. Speaking to any guard with the quest available they will ask us to go speak to the captain. He is looking for us. 
2. The player speaks to Oolga, and she describes a very complicated process and many herbs that need to be collected to fix the problem. 'you wouldn't understand. Just do what I say'. 
3. Oolga gives the player a series of menial tasks (cleaning basement, carrying letters, acquiring funds...) These tasks loop infinitely until the player `uses` any weapon on Oolga. This triggers a dialogue with Oolga where she says "no need to get testy. You are right, the herbs I need can be found in the swamp. Go get them now... good boy :P"
4. The player goes into the swamp to collect the herbs. There are plenty of monsters who are all aggressive. There are three herbs to be collected and the quest objectives lists them and updates when the player has them in their inventory. The order of collection changes the dialogue.
	1. When the first item is collected: the player also finds a bunch of broken insect eggs that are strangely deformed. 
	2. When the second item is collected: the player finds broken alchemy supplies trashed hastily in the bushes
5. When the third item is found. A rat-toad hybrid monster ambushes the player. The player can either fight or run. 
6. When we return to Oolga, the guard captain is there. Talking to either of them starts a dialogue where the captain is berating oolga for wasting time. Turns out, Oolga had everything she needed already, and she just sent you for her own personal enrichment.
7. The guard captain rewards us. 




Tasked to head out to the swamp and collect ingredients for Oolga so she can cure the mutagen. Turns out, the swamps were used as a testing ground for the ratkin's mutagen. (rat-like insects)
