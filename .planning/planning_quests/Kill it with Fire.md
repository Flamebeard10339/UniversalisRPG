`Requirements:` None
`Starting point:` Speak to Grandma Oolga
`Reward:`
Cooking experience: 1500
Access to Oolga's potion shop. 

1. Grandma Oolga will complain about how things were better back in the old days. If you ask, to see her wares, she will 'gain a glint in her eye' and refuse you until you prove that you are worthy of it. She will task you with clearing her basement of rats without killing them. 
2. Asking around reveals that the city's bar `Sha Dynasty’s` probably knows how to do this since the owner is an expert at attracting and repelling animals. 
3. Asking `Sunny` the owner, reveals that this is going to be annoying. We have to get firetouched royal jelly from a princess bee (Kelsa's apiary), and mix it with mollusk venom (border swamp) + a bottle of vodka (Sunny's bar). Then slather the corners of the basement with the stuff. 
4. The player goes out to hunt for the three ingredients. The player uses any the three ingredients on a stove while they are all in their inventory and get's `Sunny's 'poison'`
5. The player uses the poison in Oolga's basement. 
6. Turns out however, that the reason the poison repels rats is because it attracts something far worse. The player must defeat the groundwurm that appears. 
7. Speaking to Oolga after the groundwurm is defeated gives us a snarky dialogue, and a grudging reward.
