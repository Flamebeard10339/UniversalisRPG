`Requirements:` None
`Start point:` Speak to Mouse.
`Reward: `
1000 Health experience
1500 Thieving experience

1. You speak to a boy (named Mouse) hunched over a sewer grate in the market district with a forlorn expression. When you ask him what is wrong, he tells you that he lost his ball down in the sewers. You agree to go get it for him. (ball_of_a_boy=1)
	1. Story hint: Examining the boy reveals he black eyes and twitchy ears. 
	2. Progression hint: The boy will point you to the sewer entrance around the back of the castle.
	3. 
2. You find the sewer entrance is blocked by guards who dismiss you when you approach. (ball_of_a_boy=2)
	1. Bribe the guards: 1000 coins will get you in. If you have a cooked herring in your inventory, you can haggle Larry the guard down to 200 coins.(ball_of_a_boy_bribed_guards=1)
	2. Ask around: Several people around town know of a secondary way into the sewers. 
		1. Charlie the Tramp. (always)
		2. Town Crier (ball_of_a_boy=1 or 2)
		3. Oolga the Witch (ball_of_a_boy=1 or 2)
3. When you enter the sewers you find lots of aggressive rats. 
	1. Story hint: examining the rats = "The rats are feral, missing hair and have weeping sores across their body"
	2. If the player entered the sewers through the guard entrance, there is a safe area clean of rats where they enter, otherwise, the rats ambush them. 
4. The player explores the sewers and finds the grate the boy's ball fell through. It fell into the water and got carried through a large barred door. (ball_of_a_boy=3)
	1. Progression hint: Signs on the wall give direction to the major buildings in town. The player needs to go toward the market.
	2. Progression Hint: examining the grate while (ball_of_a_boy=3) says "A grate to the surface. You can see the boy waving at you through the bars."
	3. Progression hint: If the player goes up to the surface to speak to the boy, he says "Maybe the ball fell into the water and got carried through that locked door." (ball_of_a_boy=3)
5. Unlocking the door (thieving 5) reveals a large room with 2 rat men. The door locks behind the player. 
	1. If the player kills both ratmen ((ball_of_a_boy_killed_ratmen=1))
	2. The key to unlock the door is located on a table. The door will unlock if the key is in the player's inventory. 
	3. The player may try to unlock the door with thieving to escape the encounter (in reverse=thieving 15)
6. Within the room, there will be a book which describes a procedure to convert a man into a ratman

7. Returning to the surface, the boy is gone, speaking to any guard will end the quest after we describe what we saw down in the sewers. 
	1. Bringing the information to the guard captain gives us a reward of 500 additional coins as a bounty for the information.
