`Requirements:` None
`Starting point:` Speak to Kelsa
`Reward:` 
5,000 coins
All the bees on Kelsa's property are no longer aggressive (neutral)
Ability to harvest honey peacefully from the hives

1. Speaking to Kelsa, she immediately assumes we are from the Mayor here to solve her problem. She demands we remove the infestation that's driven her bees mad. When we ask what the problem is, she scoffs and says, figure it out. What does she pay us for?! But don't you dare kill my queens. Do what you have to to the drones. 
	1. Speaking to George (Kelsa's helper) hints that the farm has been having troubles
2. Asking George, gives a more nuanced answer. They've infested one of three hives and have riled up the bees so it is hard to approach. He suggests we ask the town crier for more information. The player doesn't have to do this, but the town crier will give info about what the wasps do. 
	1. Once the quest has started. Speaking to George points us to the three hives on the far side of the property. He says he thinks it is a type of wasp that is driving the bees mad. He suggests we ask the town crier about it.  
	2. Town Crier says its a type of borer wasp. 100%. All you have to do is go in there and take care of it with a good whack or two. If you question how he can be so sure, he says that the alternative is a Korning Mind Wasp but those mind controlling beasts don't live around these parts.

4. The player investigates each hive. No matter what order they investigate the hives, the last hive is always the one containing the boss room. (The infected hive)
5. Entering the boss hive is an instanced area that resets each time the player enters. Inside, the player finds that the wasp is indeed a Korning Mind Wasp. And it has taken control over the queen. 
6. Dual boss fight where we lose if the queen bee or we die. Both the queen bee and the wasp target us and are allies to each other. The wasp will periodically cast an ability to drain life from the queen bee to heal itself. If queen/player dies we are kicked out of the arena with a dialogue from George who says "I got here just in time to drag you out. Are you alright?". We can then try the fight again as many times as we want.  
7. Speaking with Kelsa gives us the reward.
