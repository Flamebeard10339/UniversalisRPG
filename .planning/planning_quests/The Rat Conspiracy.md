`Requirements:` [[Birds and the Bees]]
`Starting point:` Speak to Kelsa
`Reward:` 5,000 Coin

1. Kelsa mentions that shes been feeling rumblings underneath the ground for a bit now. Worried about moles, she searched and found a tunnel entrance nearby. She asks you to go get permission from the duke to set off a charge and collapse the tunnel. 
2. The duke says they must invesitage what is on the other side of the tunnel before they nuke things. Kelsa says we should make the bombs to collapse the tunnel first just in case things get hairy. 
3. The player enters the tunnels which are infested with feral rats. Like the sewers. 
4. The player investigates the tunnels and finds plenty of evidence (examine text + monsters) that this is related to the rat conspiracy.  
5. The player emerges after fighting their way through the tunnels and finds a rat army on the other side. 
6. We rush back and Kelsa who tasks us with setting the charges. The player does so and the tunnels explode. (screenshake)
7. The tunnels are sealed and we are rewarded for our efforts. 



We are hired by the guard to investigate cow disappearances after getting recommended by Kelsa. We discover that the ratkin are smuggling hostile monsters into the kingdom and setting them loose. Quest ends with blowing up the smuggler tunnel they are using.
