# Miki Questline 
## Path 1 - miki route
1. Game starts with Miki greeting the player and introducing them to the new world.
  Miki asks the player if they want to learn how to be an adventurer from him. 
  (Path divergence: Player disagrees - goto path 2)
2. Miki tells the player about quests and gives them one. The first step is to check out the mirror where they can set their player name and choose their race. 
3. Miki tells the player about temporary buffs and walks the player through making bread (jug of water + pot of flour in inventory = dough, dough + oven = bread).
4. Miki then explains abouts stats and skills. He tells the player to go down into the basement and kill a few giant rats to see how it works. Miki gives the player an iron sword and wooden shield to show them how equipped items improve their stats.  
5. The player goes down to the basement and kills three giant rats. 
6. Miki congratulates the player for defeating their first foe. He admits that there is not much else that they can teach the player and that they should go out and become a true adventurer. Miki leaves the player with one last step on their 'tutorial island quest' which is to get to the mainland.  
7. The player leaves Miki's house to continue the quest. 

## Path 2 - thieving route
1. The player snubs Miki by refusing his help/advice which makes Miki mad, refusing to assist. 
  (Path divergence: Player apologizes - goto path 3)
2. If the player examines a bookshelf in the back room, a book 'calls' to them, initiating a conversation with an unknown entity as the words appear on the page. The unknown entity offers the player the same quest (escaping tutorial island). step 1: is getting out of the house (front door is locked, Miki mocks the player if they try opening it). 
3. The entity can't help the player, though the player can start a conversation whenever they want since the book is in their inventory with a 'read' action. The book encourages them to explore. 
4. The player finds a bunch of random items around the house but then find a set of worn lockpicks in a drawer on the second floor. 
  random distractors: coins, a short sword and shield, leather boots
5. The book will stop the player if they try to unlock the door. Instead, the book tells teh player that they should practice first (get to level 2) on the chest in Miki's bedroom. (thieving combat)
6. When the player gets to level 2, they make a vase fall over in another room, drawing Miki's attention and run to the door. There they attempt to unlock the door.
  If the player fails to unlock the door in 30s, their action is stopped and they have to try again. (miki returns to his location in the living room, which removes the lockpicking action)
7. The player leaves Miki's house to continue the quest. 

## Path 3 - The fishing/cooking route
1. Miki is mad at the player. 
2. Player exploration to the back garden in the house reveals a small pond and a helpful fairy called Shria. The fairy offers to teach the player about fishing. They explore to find a used fishing net and start training fishing at the pond.  
3. When the player's inventory fills up with fish or when the player has talked to the Shria after catching at least 3 shrimp. The fairy will tell the player about inventory constraints and how they can maximize their experience by going inside to cook the shrimp. The fairy tells the player about the chef's hat in the basement boxes though it warns that they might have to clear the rats if they want any peace and quiet while searching. 
4. Player cooks their shrimp
  1. The player goes down to the basement and searches the crates. The rats attack the player. (aggressive monsters). Finding and equipping a chef's hat in the basement improves cooking by +5. The player cooks their shrimp. 
  2. (ignore fairy) The player goes inside to cook their shrimp. getting some burnt results. 
  - knowledge divergence: Speaking to the Shria includes a dialogue about how skilling actions can fail if you are not skilled enough. Burnt shrimp are useless and should be just thrown out (Dropped on the ground)
5. If the player attempts to leave Miki's house at this point, (the door is still locked) Miki will talk to the player and apologize for being terse. He has been watching them, and realizes that not all quests and guided tours are for everyone. 
6. Miki still offers the player the quest, though this time he explains that it is his job and he has to give the quest to all new arrivals. It is truncated as much as he can make it. The goal: get off tutorial island. There is a boat to the mainland where there are plenty more skilling opportunities. 
7. The player leaves Miki's house to continue the quest. 

## Unrelated Information Divergences
1. The second floor contains a window where teh player can discover the nearby locations including the garden (fishing route). 
2. There is a painting in the house showing Miki wearing blue full plate armor fighting a dragon with an odd orange shield on his left hand. It is an action shot, and explains his scars on his face. 
3. The mirror is available on any path, though Path 1 requires it. 
4. There is a powerful blade above the mantle of Miki's bed. Attempting to take it (thieving route or not) will fail as the blade appears glued to the wall. 

All contained quests have the same start and end, but allow the player to 'reskin' their journey through it. Which may have lasting consequences outside of the quest.
