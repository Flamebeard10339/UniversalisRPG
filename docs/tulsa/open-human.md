## Whether a guard should jail rather than hold

The brief says a hand caught in a guard's pocket ends in jail. What ships is the same
`inflict: dazed for daze-duration` every pocket carries — four seconds of somebody's
fist in your shirt, off `# action pick-pocket` — because a `relocate:` to the barracks
ends the run and the sheet then measures the walk back rather than the guard. Jail is
the better fiction and it costs the guardsman its place on the ladder: it currently
tops the first band at 0.99× of target at level ten, and it would go to roughly what
the knight reads when it is throwing you across town.

*Moves when: you say whether the first band's top rung is meant to be farmable at all,
or whether being caught by the duke's men is supposed to end the afternoon. Either is
the guardsman's `+on unfinished:` in `content/thieving.dsl`, which is where every
per-mark consequence now sits.*
