## `entrances:` was refused this morning and asked for this afternoon

Ruled 2026-09-05: **a region names only what it holds**, and `entrances:` does not earn its
place. The case it was refused on was another module joining your region without knowing what
is inside it — a case nobody has written.

An older playtest note asks for the same keyword on a different case, and this one is real
rather than hypothetical: *define a location as being on the edge of a region, so that when
zoomed out or standing outside that region, only those nodes are visible — and the blob
bisects them rather than swallowing them.* That is a drawing problem, not a cross-module one,
and it is what makes a large region readable from outside itself.

The two are the same keyword answering to two different needs, and only one of them was put
to you.

*Moves when:* you say whether the edge-of-region case reopens `entrances:`, or whether the
drawing should work its own edge out — a place a road reaches from outside the region is an
entrance by definition, which would keep the derivation this world prefers and author
nothing.
