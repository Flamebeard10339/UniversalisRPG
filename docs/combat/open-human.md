## What a passive that is not one flat grant of one stat should do

Sixty of seventy-five passives are one grant of one stat and are now derived from a budget.
The other fifteen are not, and were measured rather than guessed: **seven trade-offs**
(`reckless: +10% physical-damage, -2 defense`), **four that scale per resource or stack**
(`+2% physical-damage per rage`), **three behaviour-only** with no stat grant at all, and
**one granting two stats**.

Two of those answer themselves and are already right: a passive granting no stat declares no
budget and is audited against nothing, and a two-stat passive is two grants. What has no
obvious answer is what a trade-off's *give* costs against its *take*, and what a
`per rage` clause is worth when the worth depends on rage held rather than on the ladder.

*Moves when: he says whether a trade-off is budgeted on its positive side alone, and whether
a per-resource clause is budgeted at all or left hand-cut and merely reported.*
